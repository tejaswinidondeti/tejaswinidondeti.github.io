Third-Party Risk Management

FinSecure Banking — TPRM Vendor Assessment

Third-Party Risk Management assessment deliverable set for a 1,200-employee FDIC-insured bank integrating a payment processor (SwiftPay Solutions) — a vendor profile, 4-risk vendor register, verification of 6 controls across PCI DSS and SOC 2, a 3-issue remediation tracker, and a formal Conditional Approval recommendation

Company: FinSecure Banking (1,200 employees)
Industry: Financial Services · FDIC-insured
Vendor Assessed: SwiftPay Solutions (payment processor)
Frameworks: OCC Bulletin 2013-29 · PCI DSS v4.0 · SOC 2 Type II

Project Overview

FinSecure Banking is the scenario’s 1,200-employee mid-size FDIC-insured financial institution integrating a third-party payment processor, SwiftPay Solutions, into its cardholder data environment (CDE). The integration processes credit and debit card transactions for the bank’s retail and commercial customers and is subject to OCC Bulletin 2013-29 on third-party relationships, PCI DSS v4.0 requirements for protecting cardholder data, and SOC 2 Type II attestation requirements for service organizations.

Acting as TPRM Lead, this assessment produces the full vendor due-diligence deliverable set: a vendor profile evaluating SwiftPay’s security posture, a 4-risk vendor risk register with 5×5 scoring, verification of 6 security controls mapped to PCI DSS and SOC 2 (via SOC 2 Type II report, PCI Attestation of Compliance, and direct evidence requests), a 3-issue remediation tracker with owners and timelines, and a formal recommendation with contractual protection clauses.

Project Highlights

Vendor ProfileSwiftPay Solutions — payment processor with direct access to FinSecure’s cardholder data environment (CDE). Profile evaluates security posture, certifications, sub-processors, and financial stability against OCC Bulletin 2013-29 due-diligence expectations.
Vendor Risks Assessed4 vendor-specific risks on a 5×5 Likelihood–Impact matrix — (1) Cardholder Data Breach at SwiftPay, (2) SwiftPay Service Outage disrupting payment processing, (3) Regulatory Non-Compliance by SwiftPay, and (4) Fourth-Party Risk from SwiftPay’s subcontractors — each paired with a treatment plan and target residual scoring
Controls Verified6 security controls verified through SOC 2 Type II report review, PCI DSS Attestation of Compliance, and direct evidence requests: Cardholder Data Encryption, Access Control to CDE, Vulnerability Management & Patching, Incident Response & Notification, Business Continuity & DR, and Logging / Monitoring / Audit Trail
Remediation Issues3 issues identified during the assessment requiring remediation before or shortly after full production integration: (1) Incident Notification SLA Gap, (2) TLS 1.2 in transit (scenario target: TLS 1.3), and (3) Limited Fourth-Party Visibility — each tracked with owner, target date, and evidence link
RecommendationProduced a formal Conditional Approval recommendation — proceed with SwiftPay integration subject to the 3 remediation items being resolved within specified timelines; scenario notes SwiftPay’s clean PCI DSS certification and unqualified SOC 2 Type II opinion as supporting factors
Contractual Protections DesignedRequired contract clauses drafted: (a) remediation milestones as contractual obligations, (b) right-to-audit clause, (c) performance SLAs with financial penalties, and (d) termination provisions if Critical remediation items are not resolved
Frameworks AppliedOCC Bulletin 2013-29 (Third-Party Relationships), PCI DSS v4.0 (cardholder data protection), SOC 2 Type II (service organization controls), and standard 5×5 vendor risk scoring methodology
Regulatory ScopeFDIC insurance obligations, OCC third-party guidance, PCI Security Standards Council requirements for the CDE, and enterprise customer contractual due-diligence expectations
